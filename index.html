Word list

EBIT (operating profit)
What the company earns from running ships and services, before interest and tax. Positive = profit, negative = loss. In US dollars, billions ($bn).
Container / TEU
One standard 20-foot metal box — the unit shipping lines count and price in.
How full the ships are
Cargo waiting to be shipped divided by the ship space available. It is the number that drives prices most.
Market price
What a container costs to ship today on the open ("spot") market. It moves weekly.
Contract price
A price fixed months ahead with a big customer. This is why a company's profit follows the market price with a delay.
Hormuz, Red Sea, Suez
Sea routes for oil and cargo. When ships cannot use them they sail round Africa, which takes longer, costs more, and uses up ship space.
Range (8 times in 10)
The model plays the year 2,200 times with different luck. In 8 of 10 plays the result falls inside the range.
Honest limits. The link from a company's price to its profit is fitted to real reported results and is fairly reliable. The link from world cargo, ship numbers and the canal to the market price could not be fitted (no free history), so it follows an assumed pattern — trust the direction and the range, not the decimals. Maersk = ocean shipping only, not the whole group. 2026 total = the two quarters already reported + the two forecast quarters. Built on public information; not investment advice.
